Team

Overview

Rau Bistro is Calgary's first Vietnamese street food restaurant — a hidden gem serving dishes that reflect the chef-owner's personality and heritage. The goal was to create an urban brand identity that positions Rau Bistro as the city's premier hangout, fusing authentic Vietnamese street food with a contemporary Western appeal, while honouring its cultural roots.

The problem

Despite exceptional food and a loyal following, Rau Bistro lacked a cohesive visual identity that could communicate its unique positioning. The existing brand didn't reflect the restaurant's character — warm, fresh, and deeply rooted in Vietnamese culture — or compete effectively with well-branded competitors in Calgary's growing food scene.

Process

01

Brand Discovery

Worked closely with the chef-owner to understand her vision: to introduce Vietnamese street food culture locally and evoke hometown warmth for Calgary's Vietnamese community. Mapped the restaurant's personality, values, and the story behind the food.

02

Logo Concept

"Rau" means vegetable in Vietnamese. The brand character mirrors the food: fresh, balanced, and healthy. Dark green was chosen as the primary colour for its earthy, grounded feel — and because half the menu is vegetarian with fully compostable takeout materials.

03

Typography & Visual System

Typeface 'Classique Saigon' was selected for its elegance and retro/nostalgic quality — evoking old Saigon street signage. 'Be Vietnam' adds modern legibility and earthy balance alongside the colour palette.

04

Brand Application & Photography

Delivered a full brand system: apron, menus, gift vouchers, business cards, promotional posters, signage, and a website mockup. Managed all print production and conducted on-site photography of the final brand in context.
