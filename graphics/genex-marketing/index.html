Overview

GENEX is a global cooperative specializing in genetic and reproductive solutions for the dairy and beef industries. As a marketing designer, I create campaign assets, event materials, and digital advertising that communicate complex genetic science in ways that resonate with farmers and agricultural professionals.

The problem

Agricultural marketing often defaults to utilitarian layouts that don't capture the innovation behind the science. GENEX needed materials that were visually compelling and technically credible — speaking both to seasoned producers and newer farming audiences across print, digital, and event formats.

Process

01

ICC Campaign

The ICC™ index is a genetic selection tool for profitable, efficient dairy cows. I used a double exposure technique — blending a herd scene within the silhouette of a Holstein or Jersey cow — to visualize the concept behind the tagline: "She goes unnoticed, your herd stands out."

02

Event Marketing

Designed the 2024 GENEX calendar, branded apparel, Generation Tour promotional pieces, and visual presentations for annual meetings and leadership conferences.

03

Digital Advertising

Created animated GIF banner ads for Farm Journal programmatic placements and mobile web ads for Progress Dairy — adapting campaign visuals for each platform's format and audience.

04

Social Media

Produced reel content and social assets for GENEX's organic channels, maintaining brand consistency across a high-volume content calendar.
