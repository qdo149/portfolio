Overview

URUS is a global agriculture holding company founded in 2018, encompassing AgSource, Alta Genetics, and GENEX — serving dairy and beef producers with genetic and farm management tools. My role spans internal and external communications: leading the URUS Power newsletter and contributing to conference, event, and campaign materials.

The problem

A holding company with multiple brands under one roof needs communications that feel cohesive and purposeful — not a patchwork of sub-brand styles. URUS needed a consistent visual language across leadership conferences, employee-facing newsletters, and public-facing philanthropic initiatives.

Process

01

Leadership Conference

Developed visual concepts for the annual leadership conference, aligned with URUS's global mission and the theme 'Better Cows for a Better World.' Created a multi-page presentation system used across all conference sessions.

02

URUS Power Newsletter

Led graphics, content layout, and email marketing for the ongoing URUS Power internal newsletter — ensuring consistent brand voice and visual quality across each issue.

03

URUS Giving Campaign

Launched in 2023, URUS Giving distributes $25,000 to community causes aligned with company CSR values, with employees voting on recipient charities. I led key visuals, email campaigns, and the internal voting page.

04

Ongoing Brand Stewardship

Contributed to a range of promotional and event materials, maintaining visual consistency across URUS and its subsidiary brands throughout the year.
