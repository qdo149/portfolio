About

At the intersection of creativity and functionality.

I'm a product designer with a background in graphic design. I've always been drawn to the intersection of creativity and functionality, which is why I transitioned to product design. I love finding innovative solutions that blend visual appeal with problem-solving to create exceptional user experiences. Throughout my career, I've helped companies transform basic ideas into impactful products and features. My work has focused on enhancing the lives of end-users and driving business growth.

My graphic design background gives my product work a level of visual precision that’s hard to retrofit — I care as much about how something looks as how it works.

I’m currently studying software engineering to strengthen my technical foundation and expand my impact as a product designer.
